Kaggle automatically unzips files downloaded

This is the structure of the datafiles:

```txt
data/
    raw/
        accepted_2007_to_2018Q4.csv/
            accepted_2007_to_2018Q4.csv

        rejected_2007_to_2018Q4.csv/
            rejected_2007_to_2018Q4.csv

        accepted_2007_to_2018Q4.csv.gz
        rejected_2007_to_2018Q4.csv.gz
```

All files are git ignored because they exceed 100MB.
Polars is 7 times faster at reading dataframes than Pandas.
