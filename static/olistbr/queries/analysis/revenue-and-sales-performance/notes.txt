Gross Merchandise Value/Volume (GMV)
GMV = (sales price of goods) * (number of goods sold)

good sold when order approved
price has to exist, cannot be null, else cannot calculate GMV

    when price null:
        canceled 
        created

        shipped
        unavailable
    when price not null:
        approved
        delivered
        invoiced
        processing
        unavailable
        canceled
        shipped
    appears in price null and not null:
        approved
